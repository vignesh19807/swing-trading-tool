## 1. Test Objective & Setup

The objective of this deterministic test fixture is to verify the entire lifecycle of a swing trade when market conditions satisfy all quantitative and risk criteria:

5. Daily multi-day price tracking and collision handling.
6. Target exit execution upon reaching threshold.
7. Post-exit accounting and net P/L reconciliation.
8. Database persistence into isolated backtest tables.
9. Verification of REST API consumption via `GET /api/backtests/latest`.
