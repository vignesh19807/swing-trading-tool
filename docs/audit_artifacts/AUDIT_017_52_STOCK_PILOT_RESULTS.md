## 5. Statistical and Methodological Limitations

> [!WARNING]
> **Sample Size & Overfitting Caution:**
> 1. **Insufficient Sample:** A sample of $N = 1$ executed trade across 6,344 stock-sessions is **statistically insufficient** to establish the long-term expectancy, win rate, or Sharpe ratio of the trading system.
> 2. **Cash Preservation as Primary Outperformance Driver:** The +8.58% outperformance relative to the benchmark (-7.60%) was primarily driven by sitting in cash during an adverse market downturn, rather than generating massive trading profits.
> 3. **45-Day Reporting Lag Reliance:** Results depend on the 45-day reporting lag fallback for quarterly statements. While sensitivity testing in Phase 4 proved model stability across 45, 60, and 90-day lags, empirical validation requires verified exchange disclosure timestamps.
