## 1. Executive Summary & Verification Verdict

The AUDIT-037 remediation of historical financial eligibility across data engineering, pipeline access, and testing layers has been **independently verified and signed off**.

### Key Determinations:
1. **Core Look-Ahead Leak Remediated:**
   - In `backend/data_pipeline/backtesting_dataset_service.py`, `_prepare_financial_data` previously filtered quarters based on reporting period-end dates (`quarter_date <= evaluation_date`). This allowed an unannounced quarter ending March 31 to be selected on April 1, violating point-in-time constraints by 44 calendar days.
   - The remediated implementation strictly enforces:
     - Rule 1: Exclusion of any future reporting period ($T_{\text{end}} > T_{\text{eval}}$).
     - Rule 2: Point-in-time eligibility when a verified publication timestamp exists ($T_{\text{pub}} \le T_{\text{eval}}$).
     - Rule 3: 45-day statutory fallback lag when publication timestamps are missing or invalid ($T_{\text{end}} + 45\text{ days} \le T_{\text{eval}}$).
     - Chronological multi-quarter ordering by $(T_{\text{end}}, T_{\text{avail}})$, selecting the latest closed, eligible quarter.
2. **Empirical Historical Dataset Impact:**
   - Across all 52 universe stocks and 25,746 stock-date pairs in `database/swing_trading.db`:
     - **7,904 observations (30.70%)** changed from a look-ahead future quarter to the genuinely available prior quarter or `None`.
     - **1,456 observations (5.66%)** transitioned to `None` during the initial post-quarter window before 2025-Q1 reached its 45-day statutory filing lag.
     - Look-ahead leakage checks (`FAIL_FUTURE_FINANCIAL_PERIOD`, `FAIL_PRE_PUBLICATION_LEAK`, `FAIL_PRE_AVAILABILITY_LEAK`) now report **0 failures (100% PASS)** across all 52 stocks.
3. **Multi-Year Strategy Simulation Invariance:**
   - The 8-trade multi-year portfolio backtest (AUDIT-025 through AUDIT-036) generated by `scratch/execute_52stock_portfolio.py` utilized `run_signal_pipeline` from `signal_integration.py`.
   - `signal_integration.py` delegates to `financial_engine.py`, which accesses `financial_service.py` with `evaluation_date`. `financial_service.py` **already enforced** the 45-day fallback lag.
   - Consequently, all 8 trades, their entry/exit dates, prices, quantities, gross P&L (-₹891.08), transaction costs (₹332.60), net P&L (-₹1,223.69 / -1.22%), final equity (₹98,776.31), and maximum drawdown (2.35%) are **100% invariant**.
4. **Data Limitation Disclosure:**
   - All 260 quarterly financial records in `database/swing_trading.db` lack corporate filing timestamps (`publication_date = NULL`).
   - Every generated backtest record transparently records:
     - `financial_eligibility_mode = "ESTIMATED_REPORTING_LAG"`
     - `is_financial_publication_verified = False`
     - `financial_availability_status = "UNVERIFIED"`
   - The 45-day fallback is an operational assumption grounded in SEBI LODR Regulation 33, not proof of the actual filing timestamp.
